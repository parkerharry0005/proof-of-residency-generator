What is a Proof of Residency Letter?

A Proof of Residency Letter, commonly referred to as an Affidavit of Residence, is a sworn legal statement used to verify that an individual currently resides at a specific residential address. This document is frequently requested when an applicant does not have utility bills, lease contracts, or mortgage papers under their own name.

When Do You Need an Affidavit of Residence?

  • DMV REAL ID & Driver's License: State DMVs require multiple proofs of physical residence address.
  • School District Enrollment: Parents must prove residency within the school boundaries to enroll students.
  • Financial Institutions: Opening a checking or savings account requires physical address verification.
  • Government Benefits & SNAP: Proving state residency for welfare or medical assistance.

Does a Proof of Residency Letter Need Notarization?

In most US states, government institutions (like the Department of Motor Vehicles or county clerks) require an official Affidavit of Residence to be notarized. Our free generator automatically appends a standard Notary Public Jurat / Acknowledgment Block at the bottom of the generated PDF file.
